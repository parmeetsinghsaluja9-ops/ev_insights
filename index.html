Superstore Sales and Profitability Analysis

Where does a superstore make money, and where does it lose it? A Tableau dashboard and six-point data story built on 8,399 order lines from 2009 to 2012.

Objectives

  • Build a responsive, multi-chart Tableau dashboard.
  • Tell a data story with explained story points and recommendations.
  • Test performance against four factors (data volume, filters, calculated fields, visualisation count).
  • Publish to Tableau Public and embed here.

Method

  • Source CSV kept unchanged; cleaned with a Node.js script.
  • Validated Excel workbook (formulas independently recalculated).
  • Tableau specs: 12 calculated fields, 14 worksheets.
  • Static site: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Key findings from the data

Figures computed from the cleaned dataset (amounts as supplied; currency not stated by the source). The bar charts below are quick previews drawn by this page, not the Tableau visualisations.

Sales by year

Sales fell after 2009, partly recovering in 2012.

Profit margin by category

Furniture is about 35% of sales but earns a 2.3% margin.

Six biggest sub-category losses (profit)

Only Tables, Bookcases, Scissors/Rulers and Rubber Bands are negative.

Margin by ship mode

Delivery Truck: 42% of sales, 4.3% margin. Large furniture probably drives this; test before acting.

Margin by discount band

Lines with 4%+ discount earn about 8%, versus 12.6-13.6% at 0-3%. Association, not proven cause. (Band above 10% omitted: 5 lines.)

Margin by region

West is the largest region but earns 8.3%.

Recommendations

  1. Review pricing and costs for Tables and Bookcases, and for truck-shipped furniture.
  2. Check whether discounts above 3% are justified; test discount caps on a sample.
  3. Investigate West and Yukon margins (8.3% and 7.6%).
  4. Protect high-margin lines: Binders (30.0%), Labels (35.1%), Envelopes (27.7%).

About 50.8% of order lines are loss-making (total losses 1.10M against 1.52M net profit), so loss control is the largest lever.

Tableau story (6 story points)

  1. Big picture: 10.2% margin, flat sales.
  2. Furniture: high sales, low profit.
  3. Loss-making sub-categories.
  4. Shipping mode and margin.
  5. Discounts and thinner margins.
  6. Regions and recommendations.

Data source and dictionary

Dataset: superstoreSales.csv from the public curran/data repository on GitHub (a Tableau-style sample "Superstore" with Canadian provinces). Licence and original provenance are not stated in that repository; treat as an educational sample dataset.

Performance testing Results pending: not yet measured

Four factors are tested one at a time with other variables held constant, three runs per level, recording load or response time in seconds:

  1. Amount of data rendered (2,000 / 4,000 / 8,399 rows).
  2. Utilisation of filters (0 / 2 / 4 / 6 filters).
  3. Number of calculated fields (0 / 3 / 6 / 10).
  4. Number of visualisations (1 / 3 / 6 / 9 sheets).

No Tableau measurements have been taken yet, so no timings are shown. The plan is in test_plan.md; record results in results_template.csv.
